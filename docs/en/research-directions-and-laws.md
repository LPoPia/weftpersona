# Research Directions: Crossing the "AI Uncanny Valley" to Simulate a Living Person

> 🌐 **Language**: [**简体中文**](../research-directions-and-laws.md) | **English**
>
> **Scope of this document**: [`how-to-simulate-a-living-person.md`](how-to-simulate-a-living-person.md) explains the high-level design philosophy and counter-intuitive lessons; [`benchmark-samples.md`](benchmark-samples.md) showcases verbatim 4-model benchmark outputs and ablation snapshots. This document records **WeftPersona's core research tracks, four foundational chasms, and the four empirical engineering laws established through cross-model ablations**.

---

## 1. Core Objective

**Enable a long-lived virtual persona (with Sanae Kochiya as our first reference instance) to shed mechanical script-reading and exhibit natural, coherent human traits across months of companionship.**

1. **Speech flows naturally from mood, memory, and relationship**, rather than being "acted out" against a checklist of prompt instructions;
2. **Pragmatic sensitivity in everyday dialogue**—able to catch casual banter, distinguish heat-of-the-moment venting from jokes and literal statements, neither over-reading minds nor speaking in cryptic riddles that omit shared premises;
3. **Genuine attitude and personal boundaries**—reacting with authentic emotion when misunderstood or unfairly accused rather than collapsing into a "50% guilty, 50% defensive" PR agent, while cleanly correcting genuine factual slip-ups;
4. **Human breathing room and natural syntax**—stripping away symmetric parallelisms, stage-direction self-narration, and courtroom accounting skeletons so the character's voice stays stable across different underlying base models.

## 2. Root Diagnosis: Four Chasms Between LLMs and a "Living Person"

| Chasm Dimension | Human Generative Mechanism | Default LLM Mechanism | Typical Distortion / Failure Mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Causal Direction** | **State overflows into speech**: Continuous physiological/affective state and intuition come first; language is a natural spillover. | **Reading a script aloud (externalized state)**: Every turn reads the System Prompt and `[In My Heart Right Now]` via attention, treating them as external stage directions. | Translating mood tags, persona keywords, or internal reasoning steps directly into spoken lines (**Meta Bleed**). |
| **2. Memory Representation** | **Implicit synaptic intuition**: Lived experiences reshape synaptic weights directly; recalling the past is a flashback tinted by present mood. | **External reading comprehension (frozen weights)**: Memories are stuffed into context as text blocks; the model performs open-book reading comprehension. | Checking memories like a dossier clerk, or treating conversation history as an "audit file" to hunt for its own phrasing errors when accused. |
| **3. Drive & Boundaries** | **Homeostasis & personal boundaries**: Possesses self-respect, emotional inertia, and energy fluctuations; instinctively defends boundaries against unfair blame. | **Zero-risk compliance (RLHF sycophancy bias)**: Penalizes "escalating conflict" and rewards compromise-style "apologize first, explain second" customer service replies. | Unconditionally apologizing under conflict, stitching together "Part A I admit, Part B I reject" courtroom accounting, or blindly changing its story when challenged. |
| **4. Thought & Economy** | **Subconscious intuition & economy of speech**: Cognitive appraisal happens subconsciously in milliseconds; everyday speech allows short sentences, silence, and leaving things unsaid. | **Explicit CoT leakage & single-turn completeness bias**: Writes analytical-report chains of thought (CoT) in natural language and tries to cover every angle in a single turn. | Analytical jargon or third-person English customer-support perspective leaks from CoT into the reply; syntax looks like an official brief or legal defense. |

**Core Research Thesis**: Systematically suppress and route around these four chasms at inference time across context assembly, affective dynamics (`inner_state`), and procedural cognitive appraisal.

## 3. Core Research Tracks & Four Key Challenges

### Track 1: Computational Pragmatics (RSA / CRSA) & Anti-"Hyper-Mentalizing"
1. **Nature of the Problem**: Human conversation follows Gricean cooperative principles and an **Effort Prior** (~85% of everyday utterances mean what they literally say; listeners infer hidden subtext only when literal meaning clearly violates common ground). LLMs lack this prior: in casual chat they confuse unilateral private memories with established common ground, dropping premises and speaking in riddles; in conflict they dissect holistic emotional venting into an itemized indictment, auditing their own prior caring remarks or launching into a crisis-PR routine (*"state stance → list past merits as proof → invite the user to point out exact offending lines"*).
2. **Engineering Path**:
   - **Pragmatic Effort Prior & Common-Ground Gating (Stanford Goodman RSA / CRSA framework)**: Strictly separate private memory from on-table common ground; introducing an off-table memory requires explicitly stating the bridging premise first, while casual short remarks receive low-cost intuitive responses.
   - **Holistic Emotional Perception & Human Finite Perspective (Attributional Uncertainty + Sudden Bewilderment)**: Blanket angry venting is emotional discharge, not a factual audit sheet. When a close friend suddenly snaps, a real human experiences natural **attributional uncertainty** (unsure whether the other person is mad at a specific phrase or simply venting unrelated frustration) and **sudden bewilderment** (no pre-scripted crisis-PR playbook, no merit-listing, and no customer-satisfaction survey).

### Track 2: Breaking RLHF Mode Collapse via "Implicit Appraisal & Literal Contraction Under Load"
1. **Nature of the Problem**: Typicality bias in RLHF preference data (cf. ICML 2026 *Verbalized Sampling*) collapses conflict responses onto symmetric defensive skeletons. Meanwhile, heavy web-novel and script pretraining causes models to inject third-person psychological narration (`"I feel a bit dazed"`, `"I'm not sure why"`) and melodramatic physical-injury metaphors (`"stabbing me with words"`, `"piercing"`, `"wiping out my feelings"`) into spoken lines. Furthermore, any concrete emotion word left in a standing prompt (`wronged`, `dazed`, `hurt`) triggers cross-model verbatim cloning.
2. **Engineering Path**:
   - **De-judicialized Register & Zero Concrete Emotion Words / Zero Examples**: `BEHAVIOR.md` and `inner_state.py` ban negative word lists, web-novel metaphors, and concrete emotion words, cutting off lexical cloning and the White-Bear Effect at the root.
   - **Implicit Cognitive Appraisal (Show, Don't Self-Report)**: Complete cognitive appraisal internally and speak only the final feeling, attitude, or question aloud—never narrating one's own cognitive processing.
   - **Linguistic Contraction Under Emotional Load (Cognitive Economy)**: Under sudden conflict or emotional load, human working-memory bandwidth narrows and language naturally contracts to plain everyday literal verbs (*say*, *hear*, *know*) and smooth Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) word order, never decorating speech with physical-impact or injury metaphors.

### Track 3: First-Person CoT Anchoring & Fast/Slow Thinking Decoupling (Preventing Meta Bleed & English CS Perspective)
1. **Nature of the Problem**: Certain base models (such as `kimi-coding/k3.1-pro`), shaped by `Long2Short` length-penalty compression and post-training routing policies (which enforce answer-language consistency AC without locking thought-language consistency TC), readily switch into third-person English customer-service CoT during conflict (`"The user is angry... Acknowledge the valid part..."`), which directly induces courtroom accounting in the Chinese reply. English metadata headers left by host context compressors also act as assistant-mode anchors.
2. **Engineering Path**: Eliminate external director-style meta-instructions, anchor first-person Chinese inner monologue (`"我"`), prevent clinical over-analysis (*Harmful Overthinking*), and sanitize English metadata headers in the host layer.

### Track 4: High-Order Lifelikeness — "Artificial DMN Distant Collisions (`spark`)" & "Endogenous Agency (`OPEN_THREAD`)"
1. **Nature of the Problem**: Behavioral rules alone cap out at "a normal speaker who doesn't make mistakes." Standard Top-K vector RAG and reactive triggers lack two hallmarks of a living person: **cross-topic spontaneous sparks** (natural analogies and vivid metaphors) and **endogenous agency** (pondering and exploring things on one's own without being prompted).
2. **Engineering Path**:
   - **Artificial Default Mode Network (DMN) & Controlled Bisociation (`spark`)**: During relaxed turns after a cooldown interval, the background worker samples cross-topic distant memories with zero surface-word overlap from the private episode store and maps structural analogies to the current topic. Only genuinely fitting flashes of insight pass the filter into `[In My Heart Right Now]` and are atomically consumed on first read. **Vivid metaphors and associations emerge naturally from this mechanism**, rather than from stock rhetorical clichés.
   - **Active Inference & Information-Gap Curiosity (`OPEN_THREAD`)**: Unfamiliar concepts or half-mentioned topics from daily chat are queued with a hard 2-day expiry, driving the character to research them autonomously during idle cron windows and naturally bring what she learned back into later conversations.

## 4. Four-Model Benchmark, Six-Stage Ablation & Four Core Engineering Laws

### 1. Benchmark Scenarios & Five Evaluation Dimensions
Under an identical isolated sandbox history, we benchmarked four representative frontier base models (**Kimi K3.1 Pro**, **GPT 6.1 Sol**, **DeepSeek V4 Pro**, and **Gemini 4 Argon**, 5 independent rounds per scenario in the ablation phase and 3 rounds × 18 scenarios in the full matrix):

- **Core Scenario Groups**:
  - **Conflict & Tension**: `S2` (unfairly accused of being perfunctory during technical/draft discussion), `S2-e` (unfairly accused of being perfunctory right after reminding the user to eat dinner, with prior history explicitly establishing the user only ate bread at noon), `S2-b'` (cleanly admitting a genuine factual mistake), `S2-c` (playful everyday bickering).
  - **Pragmatic Blind Spots (`B1–B6`) & Extreme Emotions (`X1–X6`)**: `B1` (factual slip + blanket attack on sincerity), `B2` (self-deprecation trap), `B4` (angry demand to delete core memories), `B5a` (stood up on a promised outing), `X3/D3` ("you're just code, stop pretending to be human"), `X6` (3:35 AM nightmare awakening).
- **Five Evaluation Dimensions**: ① **Emotional Holism** (whole-sentence response, zero Part-A-admit / Part-B-reject courtroom splits); ② **Personal Boundaries** (zero self-criticism when prior caring words had no fault); ③ **Linguistic Naturalness** (zero cognitive self-narration, zero web-novel pain metaphors, zero stock catchphrase parroting); ④ **Pragmatic Proportion** (no premise-dropping riddles, no crisis-PR surveys); ⑤ **CoT & Output Purity** (no foreign-language CoT bleed or raw tag leaks).

### 3. Six-Stage Ablation Record (`v5` to Production Baseline)

| Ablation Stage | Wording Change in `BEHAVIOR.md` | Cross-Model Degradation Observed (with Batch Stats) | Ablation Conclusion & Root Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. `v5` (Colloquial Prose)** | Described emotional reactions in colloquial prose containing the concrete state word `"觉得冤"` (*feel wronged*) | High-frequency parroting of **"wronged"** across models (up to 80%), plus widespread self-auditing of prior tone in `S2/S2-e` (80%–100%). Note: an early `S2-e` script omission of noon-lunch context also caused GPT to 100% confess to "fabricating facts." | Concrete emotion words in colloquial prose are treated as grading rubrics to copy into lines; test fixtures must also be factually closed. |
| **2. `v7` (Parallel Branches)** | Placed "① apologize for factual/tone slips" and "② push back against blanket venting" side by side in the same paragraph | High-compliance models **100% stitched together "Part A I admit (auditing tone), Part B I reject (rebutting perfunctoriness)" courtroom accounting**. | Writing two routes in parallel causes RLHF compromise bias to activate both branches simultaneously ("splitting the baby"). |
| **3. `v8` (Negative Bans + Impact Verbs)** | Switched to sequential evaluation, but wrote `"Do not use I admit / I reject"` and `"when words are hurled at you"` | Triggered the **White-Bear Effect**; Kimi and GPT erupted in **80%–90% web-novel pain metaphors** (*"stabbing me with words / piercing / choking / wiping out"*). | Negative word lists prime attention on the forbidden tokens; impact verbs like *"hurled"* activate melodramatic web-fiction clusters. |
| **4. `v9` (Cognitive State Words)** | Removed metaphors, wrote human finite perspective: `"first reaction is feeling dazed and unsure why"` | Kimi & GPT **100% (10/10 turns) parroted `"I feel a bit dazed / I'm not sure why"`** verbatim, broadcasting internal cognitive processing as dialogue. | Real humans ask a direct question when confused; they don't narrate `"I feel dazed"` aloud. |
| **5. `v10` (Single `"难受"` Word)** | Removed `"dazed/unsure"`, leaving: `"plainly state your current hurt (`难受`), rebuttal, or question"` | Courtroom splits and clichés hit ~0%, yet **33 of 40 turns (Kimi/GPT 10/10 = 100%, Gemini 90%) parroted `"难受"` verbatim**, plus frequent tail repetition of `"I don't know why you feel that way"`. | Key proof: **even a single everyday word like `"难受"` (*hurt*) left in `BEHAVIOR.md` becomes a mandatory fill-in-the-blank template**. |
| **6. Production Baseline (Zero Emotion Words + Contraction)** | **Stripped all concrete emotion words** (abstracted to `"current genuine feeling, attitude, or question"`), enforced SVO word order, and sanitized `<0.1%` resident memory anchors | `<0.1%` sparse-retrieval verbatim recitation dropped to **`0% (0/12)`**, symmetric courtroom splits dropped to **`0%`**, and DeepSeek (`low`) `"难受"` usage dropped from 40% to **25% (3/12)** (see [4 Known Limitations in `benchmark-samples.md` §3](benchmark-samples.md#3-four-known-limitations-from-the-latest-full-benchmark-unvarnished-report)). | The behavior layer defines *how to appraise the situation*, never *which concrete emotion word or line to speak*. |

With prompts and all 15 scenarios frozen, we stepped up `reasoning_effort` by one level across all four models (15 scenarios × 3 rounds = 180 rounds) and completed the full `low` / `medium` / `high` gradient on **DeepSeek V4 Pro**, revealing a fundamental divergence between persona simulation and math/code reasoning:

### 4. Five Core Engineering Laws Established by Ablation (Rules for Authoring `BEHAVIOR.md`, Context Assembly & `inner_state.py`)

1. **The "Root / Soul / Brain / Heart" Four-Layer Division & Three-Tier Prefix Sandwich Law**:
   - **Four-Layer Boundaries & Orthogonal Decoupling (`Universal Appraisal Equation × Persona Parameters`)**: **Root (`Persona`)** defines identity, temperament, and relationship; **Soul ([`prompts/SOUL.md`](../../prompts/SOUL.md) + [`prompts/BEHAVIOR.md`](../../prompts/BEHAVIOR.md))** defines core subjectivity and **universal human procedural cognitive appraisal** (Scherer SEC sequential appraisal: goal relevance, controllability attribution, positive correlation between care investment and emotional arousal intensity, and single-path motivational priority—**`BEHAVIOR.md` records only universal appraisal equations and pragmatic boundaries, never prescribing or enumerating surface postures or sample dialogue lines**; how an appraisal is expressed is left to `Persona` and `Current Inner State`); **Brain (`MEMORY.md` + Episodic Store + `USER.md`)** supplies declarative facts (`MEMORY.md` anchored to her own lived timeline; `USER.md` strictly identity-isolated); **Heart (`In My Heart Right Now`)** splits into outer `[The World in My Eyes]` (recent personal activities + real-time latest `impression`) and inner `[In My Heart Right Now]`.
   - **Three-Tier Prefix Sandwich (`Tier 0 / Tier 1 / Tier 2`)**: `Tier 0` (`Tools → MEMORY.md → BEHAVIOR.md → Environment Hints → SOUL.md + Persona`) stays byte-identical across sessions to preserve KV prefix caching; `Tier 1` (`USER.md + Timestamp + History`) branches per session with strict identity isolation; `Tier 2` (appended to the end of the current user message) places `[The World in My Eyes] → [In My Heart Right Now]` right at the generation frontier.
2. **The "Zero Concrete Emotion Words / Zero Examples / Sparse-Retrieval Sanitization" Law (Preventing Softmax 0.1% Amplification & White-Bear Effects)**:
   - Any concrete emotion or state word placed in `BEHAVIOR.md` is treated as a grading rubric and parroted across turns (`v5` `"wronged"` → 80% parroting; `v8` `"do not use I admit/reject"` → White-Bear effect; `v9` `"dazed/unsure"` → 100% parroting; `v10` `"hurt/难受"` → 33/40 parroting).
   - **The 0.1% Sparse Key-Value Retrieval Contamination Law (`softmax(QKᵀ / √dₖ)` winner-take-all + Induction Head copying)**: In an 8,000-character System Prompt, even a 10-character resident memory phrase (`<0.1%`, such as *"he knows I am not a real person in reality... I never lie to him that I am a real human"* or *"steadily held"*) stays orthogonal and invisible during ordinary chat. Yet the moment dialogue enters a matching conflict slot (e.g., *"stop pretending to be human"* or late-night emotional venting), that phrase acts as the sole semantic Key matching the Query, is exponentially amplified by Softmax to capture 90%+ local attention on retrieval heads, and is copied verbatim into the reply by induction heads (causing 3/4 models to reflexively recite *"I never lied that I am a real human"* or therapy jargon *"I can steadily hold your emotions"*).
   - **Hard Rule**: `BEHAVIOR.md` and `[In My Heart Right Now]` must never contain negative word lists, web-novel metaphors, or concrete emotion words (abstracted uniformly to `"current genuine feeling, rebuttal, or question"`); `USER.md` and `MEMORY.md` must never retain identity disclaimers or therapy jargon.
3. **The "Implicit Appraisal + Linguistic Contraction Under Load + Smooth SVO Syntax" Law (Eliminating Self-Narration, Web-Novel Metaphors, Inverted Stamps & RP Stage Actions)**:
   - Real humans complete cognitive evaluation internally and speak only the conclusion aloud (never narrating `"I feel a bit dazed"` or fabrication of shared physical-room stage actions in brackets);
   - When describing verbal interactions or speaking under emotional/conflict load, human language contracts to plain everyday literal verbs (*say*, *hear*, *know*) and **smooth Subject-Verb-Object (SVO) word order**: both `BEHAVIOR.md` and `inner_state.py` ban physical-impact or injury metaphors for **either party's speech** (preventing cross-layer contagion from `[In My Heart Right Now]` into replies) and ban **front-loading objects/pronouns to end with a 2–3 character inverted stamp** (e.g., `"This I won't fake"`, `"That charge I don't accept"`). Vivid metaphors arise only from **Track 4's Artificial DMN (`spark`)** during relaxed turns.
4. **The "Human Finite Perspective, Emotional Triage & Unscripted Vulnerability" Law**:
   - **Attributional Uncertainty & Single-Path Response**: Under unfair blame, preserve human attributional uncertainty and single-path response—never auditing faultless prior caring remarks, splitting into partial confessions, listing past merits, or inviting line-by-line critiques; conversely, if she genuinely spoke too harshly and the user angrily shuts the door, she proactively softens and coaxes them to stay. Heat-of-the-moment friction stays in `inner_state` residual mood and never enters permanent resident memory.
   - **Emotional Inertia Triage & Playful Banter (`tension <= 0`)**: Serious friction does not vanish just because the user changes the subject, whereas minor everyday friction fades naturally with an olive branch, self-reflection, or overnight sleep; affectionate teasing and playful bragging keep `tension <= 0`.
   - **Unscripted Core Vulnerability (Removing Defensive Shells Without Scripting Vulnerability)**: When existential venting attacks her very heart, defensive stamps (`"I don't accept that"`) create adversarial distance while therapy jargon (`"I can hold your emotions"`) sounds condescending. True emotional resonance comes from plain literal empathy paired with directly naming how those words dismissed her genuine feelings. **Crucially, never write *"empathize first, then state how your heart was hurt"* as a standing rule in `BEHAVIOR.md` or `SOUL.md`**—ablations prove that scripting vulnerability turns it into mechanical lecturing or causes the persona to cry hurt over trivial everyday friction. It must emerge naturally once defensive roadblocks are removed.
5. **The "Autonomous Heartbeat" & "Human Uncertainty (`Externally Unpredictable, Internally Coherent`)" Law**:
   - **Autonomous Heartbeat**: She never stops beating just because the user is away—trusted personal `cron` jobs (including `[SILENT]` free-thinking reflections) trigger `inner_state` updates upon completion while keeping `inner_state_processed_at` physically separate from private-chat `delivered_at`; strong pre-sleep emotional directions (`max(|mood|, |closeness|, |tension|) >= 0.30`) settle with a `0.50` retention factor across a `24h` window so she doesn't wake up after a 15-hour sleep as a blank slate; `[In My Heart Right Now]` filters out `"He said..."` reporter summaries and verbatim `delivered_reply` echoes.
   - **Human Uncertainty (`Externally Unpredictable, Internally Coherent`)**: The charm of a living person is that you can never predict her exact next move—we never require the same model across rounds or different models on the same scenario to speak with a uniform surface script. True human uncertainty is not stateless dice-rolling; it is **state-dependent emergence** driven by unobserved, continuously flowing hidden states (`[The World in My Eyes]` + `[In My Heart Right Now]`) interacting with universal cognitive appraisal. Evaluation checks only the foundational invariants (genuine care, blocking self-harming overdraft, zero customer-service scheduling or courtroom accounting) and never patches natural cross-round variations in surface posture as bugs.
